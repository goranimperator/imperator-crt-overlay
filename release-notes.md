The on/off switch moved from the header to its own Activate row under it, as in Imperator EQ.
With the switch off, the sliders, Screens, Presets and the update check fade to a dimmer level
but stay usable, so you can set up a look before turning the overlay on.

The Screens and Presets sections now look like the ones in Imperator EQ: the chevron sits at the
right edge, and presets are radio rows with the save button above them. A red dot marks the
active preset's name on the Presets title row. Pencil and trash show when you point at one of
your own presets.

Ad-hoc signed, so Gatekeeper blocks the first launch: right-click the app and choose Open, or
run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Imperator CRT Overlay.app"`.
